Material system modeling involves a lot of uncertainty that doesn't go away just because a simulation gives you a clean-looking number — measurement error, manufacturing variance, and model assumptions all compound. The uncertainty quantification workflows here existed to make that uncertainty visible rather than hidden, running sensitivity analyses that showed which input variables actually drove variation in the simulated outcomes, rather than treating every input as equally important.

Integrating that analytical work into the existing backend and visualization tools — rather than leaving it as a standalone research script — was the part that made it actually useful to the engineering team day to day, instead of a one-off analysis that lived in a notebook and never got looked at again.
